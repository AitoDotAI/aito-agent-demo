# The first-visit tour

A phone visitor who lands on a deep link (`/sales/`, `?view=company-data`) sees one view and a ☰ button; the menu with the other views is behind it. The tour is three skippable steps, shown once: what this is (or, on a deep link, "You are on <view>"), the menu (opened as the drawer on a phone), and the Aito panel (its button where the panel is a drawer).

- **Rules:** `frontend/lib/tour.ts`, without React, tested by `frontend/tests/tour.test.mts` (`./do frontend-test`). Ported from aito-ecommerce-demo PR #199: `autoStart`, `choseAView` and `escapeClosesTour` are its rules unchanged. The seen flag is `localStorage` `agent.tourSeen`, set when the tour starts; where storage is missing or throws, the tour does not start by itself, and the page works as before. `?demo=` (a presented demo) does not start it. This demo has no server DEMO_MODE, so there is no server answer to wait for.
- **Card:** `frontend/components/shell/Tour.tsx`, rendered by `AppShell`, styled at the end of `globals.css` (`.tour-card`). On a phone it sits above the Aito button, and on the menu step the drawer is cut short by the card's height. A phone turned sideways (760 px or narrower, 520 px or lower) puts the card beside the drawer; at 761 to 1180 px the menu is on screen and the card sits beside it, clear of the Aito button.
- **The Aito panel's preference:** on a wide desktop with the panel collapsed, the Aito step opens it with `setPanelOpen`, not the edge tab's toggle, so `rcPanelCollapsed` is never written; after the step it is collapsed again unless the visitor changed it during the step: hid it, or hid and re-showed it (`reCollapsePanel`).
- **Short phones** (760 px or narrower, 521 to 740 px tall, e.g. 375x667): on the menu step the card drops its text (the title carries the count) and moves to the bottom edge over the dimmed Aito button, so the drawer keeps 9 or more views in view.
- **Resize:** the Aito step follows the width across 1180 px (`targetFor`): the panel where it is on screen, its button where it is a drawer; a panel the tour opened is put back on the way down and shown again on the way up.
- **Focus:** when the tour ends, focus goes back to what started it, except "Take the tour" in a phone's drawer, which the tour closed: then the ☰ button (`focusAfter`).
- **Ends:** a tap on a view in the menu ends the tour (`chose_a_view`); closing the drawer with the backdrop or ☰ moves to the next step; going to another view ends it (`left_the_view`); Escape ends it when no other dialog is open; "Take the tour" during a tour closes the running one as `tour_skipped` with reason `restarted` before starting over (`restartEvent`).
- **Again:** "Take the tour" at the foot of the menu fires `agent:start-tour`.
- **View count:** `AppShell` passes it from `PAGE_TITLE` less the support view until `/api/support/status` answers, and the steps are worded at each render, so the count follows the menu.
- **Browser checks:** `frontend/scripts/tour-check.cjs` drives the tour in Chrome against a served static export (see its header); `./do frontend-test` and `tsc` run in CI (`.github/workflows/frontend.yml`).
- **Events:** `tour_started` (view, auto), `tour_step`, `tour_skipped` (at_step, reason), `tour_completed`; every event now carries `device` (`mobile` at 760 px or narrower at the first event of the page load, else `desktop`; a reload measures again).
